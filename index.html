Meet the Founder

From a Friend's Slow Winter to a Proven Formula

Turnkey started because a close friend — a talented remodeler — nearly lost his business in a slow January. His work was flawless. His phone was silent.

So I dug into one question: what actually makes a homeowner choose one remodeler over another when the quotes are similar? The answer wasn't more leads. It was being the company that looked credible before the first call — and having a system to follow up with the homeowners who weren't ready yet.

That became the framework we run today. We don't take on dentists, gyms, or law firms. We work with home remodelers, and only home remodelers, because the buyer journey, the job sizes, and the trust signals are nothing like any other industry.
