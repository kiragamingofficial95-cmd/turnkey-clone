FAQ

Questions remodelers ask us

If yours isn't here, ask it on the call - we'd rather answer honestly than take on a project that isn't a fit.

Do you only work with remodelers?
Yes. We build for home remodelers, renovation contractors and small builders. Knowing your buyer, your seasons and your objection list means the site is right the first time instead of after three rounds.
How long does it take?
Most remodeler sites launch about three weeks after the kickoff call, assuming photos and feedback come back within a couple of days at each review.
Who writes the copy?
We do, in your voice. You answer questions about how you work, what a typical project costs and what homeowners worry about - we turn that into the site.
Can you use my project photos?
Yes, and we'd rather use yours than stock. If the photos aren't strong enough, we'll tell you exactly which projects to reshoot and what angles we need.
Will I rank on Google?
We build proper structure, service-area pages and fast pages, and we set up your Google Business Profile properly. Rankings also depend on competition and reviews, so we won't promise a position - we'll show you what we do and track it.
What does it cost?
One fixed project price, agreed in writing before we start - no hourly billing and no surprise invoices. We'll quote on the first call once we've seen your scope.
